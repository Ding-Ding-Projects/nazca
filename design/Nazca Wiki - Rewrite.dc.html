RAIL OPERATOR · LOS SENGAS

Nazca Railway (Los Sengas Division)

納斯卡鐵路 (洛聖加分部) · formerly Metropolis Division

Nazca Railway Los Sengas Division is established on March 15 2015. Development of this division begun on March 10 the same year, and is currently the largest rail transport operator in Los Sengas.

Rail networks under this flagship are scattered all across the territory, and this division also assisted in multiple infrastructural projects, including but not limited to the Metro-Vector-Paw Container Terminals and Mayler Airport not in corpus.

Businesses

In Los Sengas, Nazca Railway is responsible for the construction and management of eight railway systems — from Standard Railway heavy rail down to The Shuttle personal rapid transit.

  • Standard Railway — high-capacity heavy rail, 43 lines
  • Veloduct — commuter rail, 10 routes
  • Local Rapid Transit — regional rail, 3 systems
  • Sava Light Transit — formerly operated as a subsidiary

Standard Railway System

The system includes 43 lines of Standard Railway standard — one express line, five branch lines, and thirty-seven main lines — plus three lines of Sub-Standard Railway standard.
