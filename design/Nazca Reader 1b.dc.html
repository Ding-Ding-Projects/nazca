DIVISION · RAIL OPERATOR

Nazca Railway
Los Sengas Division

納斯卡鐵路 (洛聖加分部) · formerly Metropolis Division · established 15 March 2015

Standard Railway · 43 Veloduct · 10 Shuttle · 9 LRT · 3 systems 5 media deferred

Development of this division begun on March 10 the same year, and it is currently the largest rail transport operator in Los Sengas. Rail networks under this flagship are scattered all across the territory, and this division also assisted in multiple infrastructural projects, including but not limited to the Metro-Vector-Paw Container Terminals and Mayler Airport.

Sava Light Transit (SLT) System

SLTs are also named “in-district” railways and were operated by Sava Light Transit as a subsidiary in Los Sengas. While it used to be part of Nazca Railway, it now operates independently.

Incidents

29 Apr2017 Upper Rover Station destruction incidentMisuse of administrator commands nearly destroyed the station. Emergency repair works followed backup blueprints and restoration completed the next day. The incident later inspired the station’s characteristic.
7 Jul2018 · 21:50 Glacier Station destruction incidentThe station closed for over three hours; the line then running only to Ingress Flatland Station was put out of service and another line terminated at Spike End Station. A public notice followed twenty minutes after the accident, restoration completed at 1am, and the administrator involved issued a letter of apology.

Future Development

Nazca Railway will build more stations and lines, and enhance existing services, in the process of becoming the largest Minecraft railway system in the world.
