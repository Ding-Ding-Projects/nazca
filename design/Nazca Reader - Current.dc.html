Nazca Railway (Los Sengas Division)

Current article body captured from the source at revision 132458. This reader is a static, sanitized presentation.

Read Source and attribution History boundary

Nazca Railway Los Sengas Division (納斯卡鐵路 (洛聖加分部) ;Formerly named Metropolis Division), is established on March 15 2015. Development of this division begun on March 10 the same year, and is currently the largest Rail Transport Operator in Los Sengas. Rail networks under this flagship is scattered all across Los Sengas, and this division also assisted in multiple infrastructural projects, including but not limited to the Metro-Vector-Paw Container Terminals and Mayler Airport.

Businesses

In Los Sengas, Nazca Railway is responsible for the construction and management of the following railway systemsRailway 納斯卡鐵路－關於</ref>:

  • <s>Light Rail: Sava Light Transit (Formerly)</s>
  • Regional Rail: Local Rapid Transit (LRT)
  • Medium-Capacity Rail Systems: Sub-Standard Railway
  • High-Capacity/Heavy Rail Systems: Standard Railway
  • Commuter Rail: Veloduct
  • Intercity Rail: Linker Railway (Proposed)
  • Airport Railway: Hub Railway (Proposed)
  • Personal Rapid Transit: The Shuttle

Standard Railway System

The Standard Railway System in Los Sengas Division includes 43 Lines of Standard Railway standards (1 Express Line and 5 Branch Lines, the other 37 lines are "Main Lines") and 3 Lines of Sub-Standard Railway Standards (1 Main Line, the other 2 are branch lines) connecting across Los Sengas.

Rolling Stocks

  • N-Stock: The Rolling Stock used for Standard Railways and occasionally Veloducts.
  • V-Stock: The Rolling Stock used exclusively by Veloducts.
  • H-Stock: The proposed Rolling Stock used for Hub Railways.
  • L-Tram: The Rolling Stock used by SLT, with a high undercarriage design.
  • Nick-train: The inspection train used by Nazca Railway. Also used for new routes for testing purposes.
